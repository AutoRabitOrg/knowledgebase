# Upcoming Salesforce Authentication Changes for New AutoRABIT Guard Connections

## Overview&#x20;

We're making updates to how AutoRABIT products connect to Salesforce.&#x20;

These changes are part of our ongoing efforts to improve reliability, simplify account management, and create a more consistent experience across AutoRABIT products as a Salesforce partner on AgentExchange.&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
Existing AutoRABIT Guard connections will continue to operate normally. This initial rollout only impacts new Salesforce org connections created after October 9th 2026.
{% endhint %}

## &#x20;Why am I receiving this notice?&#x20;

AutoRABIT is updating Salesforce connectivity requirements as part of our Salesforce AgentExchange partnership and Salesforce's evolving authentication standards. We're providing advance notice of an upcoming authentication change for new Salesforce org connections created within AutoRABIT Guard.&#x20;

## What is Changing&#x20;

Beginning October 9th 2026, new Salesforce org connections created in AutoRABIT Guard will be established through AuthHub. Existing connections are not changing as part of this phase. \
&#x20;\
For customers who need to take action, the process will be guided and supported by AutoRABIT. The goal is to make the transition as seamless as possible while preserving your existing configurations and settings.&#x20;

## Why Are We Making This Change?&#x20;

AutoRABIT is expanding its partnership with Salesforce through AgentExchange. As part of Salesforce's requirements for partners on the platform, we're updating how Salesforce connections are managed across our products.&#x20;

As part of our AgentExchange strategy, AutoRABIT is standardizing how Salesforce connections are authenticated. This phase introduces Auth Hub for new connections while preserving existing customer configurations.&#x20;

## What Does This Mean for My Organization?&#x20;

For most customers:&#x20;

* Your existing Salesforce org connections will continue to work normally.&#x20;
* New Salesforce org connections created after October 9th will use AuthHub&#x20;
* Your project history and settings will be retained.&#x20;
* AutoRABIT will provide guidance if any action is needed from your team.&#x20;

## Do I Need to Do Anything?&#x20;

If you are not creating a new Salesforce org connection, no action is required. Customers creating new Salesforce org connections after October 9th will follow the new Auth Hub setup process.&#x20;

Your Customer Success Manager will also be available to help answer questions and guide you through the process.&#x20;

## When Will This Happen?&#x20;

Beginning October 9th 2026, all new Salesforce org connections in AutoRABIT Guard will use Auth Hub.&#x20;

## Frequently Asked Questions&#x20;

### Will my existing AutoRABIT setup change?&#x20;

No. Your existing configurations, settings, and historical information will remain intact.&#x20;

### Will users need to be retrained?&#x20;

We do not expect significant changes to normal product usage for most customers.&#x20;

### Will there be downtime?&#x20;

We do not anticipate disruption to normal operations as part of this new process.&#x20;

### Who should I contact if I have questions?&#x20;

Please contact your Customer Success Manager or AutoRABIT Support team.&#x20;

## Need Help?&#x20;

We're committed to making this transition as smooth as possible. If you have questions about how these changes may affect your organization, please contact your Customer Success Manager.
