# Upcoming Salesforce Authentication Changes: Migrating to AuthHub

## Overview&#x20;

We're making updates to how AutoRABIT products connect to Salesforce.&#x20;

These changes are part of our ongoing efforts to improve reliability, simplify account management, and create a more consistent experience across AutoRABIT products as a Salesforce partner on AgentExchange.&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
Most customers will not experience any disruption, and many will not need to take any immediate action.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

AutoRABIT is updating Salesforce connectivity requirements as part of our Salesforce AgentExchange partnership and Salesforce's evolving authentication standards. Some customers may need to migrate their Salesforce org connection to continue using supported authentication methods in the future.&#x20;

## What is Changing&#x20;

Over the coming months, AutoRABIT will begin transitioning customers to a new connection management experience for Salesforce.&#x20;

For customers who need to take action, the process will be guided and supported by AutoRABIT. The goal is to make the transition as seamless as possible while preserving your existing configurations and settings.&#x20;

## Why Are We Making This Change?&#x20;

AutoRABIT is expanding its partnership with Salesforce through AgentExchange. As part of Salesforce's requirements for partners on the platform, we're updating how Salesforce connections are managed across our products.&#x20;

While some customers may need to complete a migration, the goal is to keep your existing product setup intact while ensuring continued compatibility with Salesforce's authentication requirements.&#x20;

## What Does This Mean for My Organization?&#x20;

For most customers:&#x20;

* Your existing product configurations will remain unchanged.&#x20;
* Your project history and settings will be retained.&#x20;
* Your day-to-day use of the product should not change significantly.&#x20;
* AutoRABIT will provide guidance if any action is needed from your team.&#x20;

## Do I Need to Do Anything?&#x20;

Not right now.&#x20;

If your organization needs to complete any steps as part of the transition, we'll notify you in advance and provide detailed instructions. Your Customer Success Manager will also be available to help answer questions and guide you through the process.&#x20;

## When Will This Happen?&#x20;

We are beginning customer communications now and will be rolling out changes in phases over the coming weeks. The initial rollout is planned for October 2026.&#x20;

## Frequently Asked Questions&#x20;

### Will users need to be retrained?&#x20;

### Will there be downtime?&#x20;

We do not anticipate disruption to normal operations as part of this transition.&#x20;
